## Context

What forces are in play. Constraints, requirements, the thing that is
unknown or contested. Written so a reader who knows nothing about the
project can follow it. No decisions here.

## Decision

The choice, stated plainly and in the present tense: "The gateway
acknowledges a request only after...". Numbered if there is more than one.

## Alternatives considered

Each option that was genuinely on the table, and the specific reason it
was not chosen. "Rejected because X" beats "we preferred the other one".

## Non-goals

What this decision deliberately excludes, so a reader can tell a choice
from an omission.

## Consequences

The costs accepted, the obligations this creates elsewhere, and the
limits of the decision. Honest about what could still go wrong.

## How this is verified

The test, benchmark or check that proves the decision holds. A ticket
reference if one owns it.
